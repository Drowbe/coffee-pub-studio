CP Tavern

SIGNED IN

Coffee Pub Tavern is the voice and video server for the people at your table. Sign in with an admin account and the CP Tavern tab lists everyone; publish a player to give them their own OBS Browser Source.

Participant Source

Their video, or a still image when the camera's off — borders and overlays are set on the Tavern. Audio routes through OBS's own Control audio via OBS toggle.

Character Source

Their character image, transparent until they talk or mute — good for overlaying a character bar.

Someone who's stepped into an ordinary aside gets muted while Enable Asides is on, so their conversation there doesn't bleed into the stream — their source stays on screen either way. Offline sources are left alone; there's no live audio to mute. No visual dim or tint on the Studio side; that's the Tavern server's call now.
