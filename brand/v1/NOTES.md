# Coffee Pub Studio - brand directions

Palette from site tokens: cream #FBF6EC, navy #14243B, teal #0B7A84, coral #F26B52 (dark theme: #F4ECDD, #5CC8CF, #FF8264 on #0E1A2B). Text is outlined (Fraunces 700 / Figtree 600). Every SVG has a `-dark` twin for navy backgrounds. Six product marks per direction: Collaborator, Blacksmith, Squire, Minstrel, Cartographer, Studio app.

**Dir 1 - Mug Top.** A cup seen from above: navy ring and handle with a teal disc and a coral drop (the "provocation"). Product marks keep ring, handle and disc fixed; the variable part is a small two-colour glyph floating on a pale-teal "coffee" disc. Wordmark sits beside it, "Coffee Pub" in Fraunces over tracked STUDIO. Weakness: the glyph is only about 11px at 16px, so products read as "mug with a dot"; the family looks like generic app-icon-in-a-circle.

**Dir 2 - Cup & Rising.** A side-view cup on a saucer with a coral spark rising where steam would be. Product marks keep the cup and saucer; the spark is replaced by the product glyph rising out of it, so the tagline ("provoke what's possible") is the system. Stacked lockup. Weakness: the glyph is the only variable and is small; the cup eats half the canvas, so at 16px the products are nearly identical cups. Also the most literal coffee imagery.

**Dir 3 - Doorway.** A solid arched door with the opening cut out and a coral sun rising in it ("the door is open"). Products are the same arch, filled in a category colour (platform navy, Foundry modules teal, apps coral) with the glyph knocked out of it. Horizontal lockup. Weakness: a single-colour knockout limits glyph detail, and the arch is a common shape, so ownability rests on colour and execution. Strongest small-size legibility.

**Dir 4 - Tile Quartet.** A 2x2 block of geometric tiles: navy and teal quarters and circles plus one coral circle. Products keep the grid and the coral circle in the top-right; the other three tiles are chosen from a shape kit (square, circle, quarter, half, diamond, triangle), so 16 products are 16 recipes with no illustration. Single-line lockup. Weakness: the marks are abstract; nothing tells Minstrel from Squire except memorised shapes, and it feels more design-studio than cozy.

**Dir 5 - Pub Sign.** A hanging pub sign: beam, two chains and a rounded board with a Fraunces "C" and a coral dot. Products keep the sign and take a category-coloured board with a two-letter monogram. Stacked lockup. Weakness: the letters collide as the portfolio grows (Collaborator/Cartographer/Studio need workarounds) and two-letter type is mush at 16px. Warm and on-theme, but the least scalable and most like a stock pub logo.

## Ranking
1. Dir 3 Doorway - best small-size legibility, a story ("every product is a door"), colour-coded categories, scales with only a glyph to draw.
2. Dir 4 Tile Quartet - the most scalable with zero illustration, but cold and abstract.
3. Dir 2 Cup & Rising - best tagline fit and warmth, but weak at 16px.
4. Dir 1 Mug Top - pleasant, generic.
5. Dir 5 Pub Sign - on-theme, but monograms collide and fail small.

**Recommendation: Dir 3 Doorway.** Next step: redraw the six glyphs by hand (they are placeholder-grade primitives) and test colour-per-category against the 16-product list.
