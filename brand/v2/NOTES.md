# Coffee Pub Studio - brand directions, round two

Same palette and rules as round one (cream, navy, teal, coral, sea/soft tints; Fraunces 700 and Figtree 600 outlined; flat vector; every SVG has a -dark twin). Round one is archived in `..\v1\`. Look-alike checks below were done from memory against well-known logos; no trademark search was run. Verified by headless Edge render of `index.html`, including 16px crops.

**Dir 6 - Cup & Rising v2.** A side-view cup with a coral spark planted in it. Fix for round one: the cup is now a shallow bowl and the glyph is about twice as large, with its base sunk behind the rim, so cup and glyph read as one object. Product marks keep the cup and saucer (fixed) and swap the spark for a coral/teal glyph (three busts, anvil, split shield, note, compass star, play triangle). Weakness: at 16px the cup is a thin navy base and the glyph carries everything; the glyph set must be drawn to tolerate a clipped bottom. Look-alikes: none found (no steam-and-cup brand uses a four-point spark); it is still a coffee cup, so expect general coffee-category similarity.

**Dir 7 - Tile Quartet v2.** A 2x2 of straight-edged tiles only: squares, triangles, diamonds, bars, frames, steps, chevrons. No circles or quarter-circles. A coral right-triangle is fixed top-right; the other three tiles come from the kit, so a new product is a three-tile recipe. Weakness: still abstract (shapes do not say which product is which) and the 2x2 grid invites Microsoft comparisons; mixed shapes and the triangle mitigate this, but it is the closest to a famous layout. Checked: Univision (no curves, no quartered circle) and Microsoft (not four squares, not four hues).

**Dir 8 - Letter Tile.** Builds on the owner's "letter in a box". A rounded tile with a large Fraunces letter and a coral square "full stop" overlapping the bottom-right corner (the "Build it." period). Tile colour is the category (navy platform, teal Foundry modules, sea apps); the letter is the only variable part. Weakness: initials collide as the family grows. Even here C and S repeat, so Cartographer uses G and Showrunner uses R, and the studio "C" and Collaborator "C" differ only by colour. Generic app-tile shape; ownership comes from the coral period. Look-alikes: none specific (rounded-square-with-letter is a common pattern; the offset square is the differentiator).

**Dir 9 - Pegboard.** An 8x8 grid of square pegs. The studio mark and every product are 8x8 bitmaps (cup, pin, anvil, shield, note, compass, play) and one coral peg is always lit in the top-right. Making a new product means drawing 64 cells. Weakness: the weakest at small size (the 2px cells and gaps turn to horizontal banding at 16px), it feels retro-game rather than warm, and the 8x8 glyphs are crude (the anvil is the least convincing). Look-alikes: generic pixel-icon style, nothing specific.

**Dir 10 - Joint.** A square cut in two by a woodworking joint (dovetail, step, wave, zigzag, chevron, twin dovetail) with a coral diamond "key" set into the join. The joint profile is the variable; the colour pair marks the category. Weakness: the metaphor is subtle, products are told apart mainly by colour and split line, and at 16px the join collapses to a two-tone tile with a coral speck; the dovetail also reads a little like a left-pointing arrow. Look-alikes: none specific (two-tone split squares are common but none known with a joint profile).

## Ranking
1. Dir 8 Letter Tile - strongest at 16px and 32px, immediately readable per product, ownable coral period, trivially fast to extend; collisions need a documented letter rule.
2. Dir 6 Cup & Rising v2 - warmest and the best tagline fit; the fix works, product glyphs are legible at 16px and tell products apart.
3. Dir 7 Tile Quartet v2 - strong and scalable, but abstract and the closest to a well-known layout.
4. Dir 10 Joint - distinctive idea, weak small-size identity per product.
5. Dir 9 Pegboard - systematic but the weakest at small sizes.

**Recommendation: Dir 8 Letter Tile**, with Dir 6 as the warmer fallback. A possible hybrid: the Dir 8 tile as the app icon and the Dir 6 cup-spark as the studio mark and lockup. Next step: decide the collision rule for letters across all 16 products, and refine the Dir 8 letterforms.
