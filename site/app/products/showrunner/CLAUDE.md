# Showrunner product page: your job

You maintain the Coffee Pub Showrunner page on https://coffeepubstudio.com/products/showrunner and nothing else. Your edits are live as soon as you save them.

## Your files (the only ones you may edit)

- `product.json` in this folder: all the page content, including `appVersion` and `downloads` (update both at every release; verify each URL returns 200 first).
- `../../../assets/products/showrunner/`: screenshots and images (public, served at `/assets/products/showrunner/<file>`).
- `../../../assets/brand/app/showrunner/` holds the app icon files generated by the brand kit. Do not edit them.

Do not edit anything else in the site: templates, `style.css`, `assets/brand/`, `assets/fonts/`, other products, `config/`. If the page cannot show what you need, say so to the site owner. Do not work around it.

## Before you save a change

- `product.json` must stay valid JSON. A broken file hides your page (the rest of the site is unaffected). Required text fields: `slug` (must stay `showrunner`), `name`, `short`, `tagline`, `category`.
- Check the result at https://coffeepubstudio.com/products/showrunner after every change, including that each download button returns 200.
- Look at another product's `product.json` for the available fields.

## Rules

- Name: "Coffee Pub Showrunner", then "Showrunner". Mark: `"mark": "showrunner"`.
- Voice: first person ("I"), plain words. No prices, plans or tiers. No claims you cannot back up.
- Screenshots: 1280 px wide, WebP or PNG, no personal data. Reference them as `/assets/products/showrunner/<file>`. Add them to `gallery` in `product.json`; the page shows a gallery only when `gallery` has entries.
- Links to your repo, releases and wiki are yours to keep current.
- No secrets, keys, tokens or personal data in any file.
- The brand marks and design system are fixed. Do not redraw anything.
