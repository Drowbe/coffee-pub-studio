What it is for

Name things the way your group does. A space can be a trip, a campaign or a team.

Trips

Plan the trip with the people coming. Put flights and stays on the plan, drop the places you like on the map, vote on where to eat, and split the costs at the end.

  • A plan, places and a map in one space
  • A calendar for the dates that matter
  • Polls for the choices, a budget for the sums

Game nights

One space for each campaign, with side conversations for whispers and a calendar for session nights. For a stream, every player is an OBS source: their camera on a clear background, with a border that lights up when they talk.

  • A chat that stays open between sessions
  • Every player as a browser source for OBS
  • Notes and research your group can find again

Projects and mentoring

Plan a product or design project with the people on it, and talk the problem through. Keep the tasks, the dates and the research in one space. Or run a mentoring group: meet for each session, vote on what to cover next, and keep the notes.

  • Chat, and a call for working things out
  • To-do, the plan and a calendar for the steps and dates
  • Polls and research your group can find again

Or anything else your group does together.

How it works

  1. 1

    Get an environment

    Its own address, its own sign-in, its own people. Nothing in it is shared with anyone else's.

  2. 2

    Make spaces in it

    A space is a place to meet: a chat, a call when you want one, and the modules you turn on for it. A family might have one, a company one per team, a game night one per campaign.

  3. 3

    Share a link

    Members sign in. Guests can come into a space with just a name, when you allow it.

The owner sets the name, the look, the people, the spaces and the modules. The servers, the call service, the updates and the backups are ours. Or run the whole thing yourself: the same software is open to self-host.

Yours, and private

No ads, and nobody selling what you say. Your people, plans and files live in your environment and are not shared with anyone else's. You can run it on your own server if you would rather.
