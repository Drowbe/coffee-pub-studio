What you get

Everything a group needs to plan a trip together, and to be on it together, in one place.

A chat, and a call when you want one

Chat for the whole trip, any day of the week. When talking is not enough, start a call with voice and video. Step into a side conversation and come back. Pop the call out into its own window. The chat is there whether or not you are on the call.

An itinerary everyone can see

In a trip, the Planner is called the Itinerary. It lays the trip out day by day: what you know, what your options are, what you have decided. Flights, stays, stops, ideas between days, and the cost of each.

Money without the awkward bit

Costs planned and paid, a target, and who owes whom. Add a cost from the itinerary and it is counted once.

Dates and to-dos, together

One calendar for the group, a month, a week or a list, with a to-do list beside it. Give a task a day and it lands on the calendar.

Decide, then move on

Ask the group: pick one, pick any, suggest another option. The result can carry straight onto the itinerary.

What a trip comes with

Each of these is a module. It can sit beside the chat, float over it or open in its own window.

  • ItineraryThe trip day by day, with what it costs.
  • PlacesThe places that matter, with an address, a position and a note.
  • MapsEverything with a position on one map, with regions kept on the server for when you are offline.
  • ResearchNotes, links and photos, tagged and shared.
  • CalendarThe group's dates, with the events of every trip you are on.
  • BudgetWhat you spend, what you planned, who owes whom.
  • PollsPick one, pick any, or suggest another option.
  • To-doTasks with due dates, linked to what they are about.
  • StreamEvery person as an OBS source, on a clear background.

And more you can turn on, such as the Assistant, which answers questions about anything the group holds.
