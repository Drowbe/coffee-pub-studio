What you get

Everything a group needs to run a campaign together, and to play it together, in one place.

A chat, and a call when you want one

Chat for the whole campaign, any day of the week. When talking is not enough, start a call with voice and video. Take a few players into a whisper and come back. Pop the call out into its own window. The chat is there whether or not you are on the call. The Common room is where everyone in the group meets, whichever campaign they are in.

A timeline everyone can see

The Campaign planner lays the campaign out on a timeline, day by day. The recap of last session sits on its day, where the next game master or player will look for it.

Every player on stream

Each player is an OBS source on a clear background. Show their video, or a Character picture that stands in for them. Put your players on a stream, or over a map, with no editing.

What a campaign comes with

Each of these is a module. It can sit beside the chat, float over it or open in its own window.

  • SessionsThe Calendar: when you next play, with the dates of every campaign you are in.
  • Campaign plannerThe campaign on a timeline, day by day.
  • QuestsTasks with due dates, linked to what they are about.
  • LoreNotes, links and pictures, tagged and searchable. Once the game master sets up the AI service, you can ask it about them.
  • PollsPick one, pick any, or suggest another option.
  • StreamEvery player as an OBS source, on a clear background.

And more you can turn on, such as Places and Maps, for the world your players move through.
